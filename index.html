A taste of place

More than food.
It's our heritage.

CALABARZON brings together five provinces, each with its own beloved delicacies and food traditions. From savory noodles to sweet coconut pastries and rice cakes, these local flavors offer a delicious way to experience Filipino culture.

Our Mission

To showcase CALABARZON's diverse cuisine, preserve culinary traditions, promote local delicacies, and introduce the region's authentic flavors to the world.

Our Vision

To become a platform celebrating CALABARZON's culinary heritage, inspiring appreciation for its local flavors, traditions, and Filipino cultural identity.

What we stand for

Our Core Values

These values guide us in sharing local flavors, respecting cultural traditions, and promoting the culinary heritage of CALABARZON.

Authenticity

We celebrate the genuine flavors, traditions, and stories that make CALABARZON unique.

Cultural Heritage

We value the preservation of local recipes, food traditions, and cultural identity.

Community Support

We recognize local food businesses, farmers, and communities that keep regional cuisine alive.

Sustainability

We encourage responsible food tourism and appreciation of local resources and ingredients.

Creativity

We present CALABARZON's culinary destinations in engaging ways that inspire discovery.

Passion for Food

We share our love for local cuisine and encourage people to explore the region's flavors.

Cavite
01 / COASTAL CLASSIC

Pancit Pusit

This striking noodle dish gets its deep black color from squid ink. Garlic, onion, and seasonings build its savory seafood flavor, often finished with chicharon and herbs.

Food story
Rooted in Cavite's coastal food culture.
Where to try
Local eateries in Cavite City, including Asiong's Carinderia.

Laguna
02 / SWEET FAVORITE

Buko Pie

This much-loved pastry pairs a golden, flaky crust with a milky filling packed with tender young coconut meat. It is a popular pasalubong and a sweet taste of Laguna.

Food story
Closely associated with Los Baños, Laguna.
Where to try
Pasalubong shops, bakeries, and roadside stops around Laguna.

Batangas
03 / COMFORT IN A BOWL

Batangas Lomi

Thick, comforting soup made with fresh miki noodles in a rich broth. It is commonly topped with ingredients such as kikiam, pork, liver, egg, and spring onion.

Food story
Widely associated with Lipa City and Batangas food culture.
Where to try
Lomi houses in Lipa City and nearby towns.

Rizal
04 / TRADITIONAL KAKANIN

Suman

A classic Filipino rice cake made from glutinous rice, steamed or cooked and wrapped in leaves. Its texture is sticky yet soft, with a gentle flavor.

Food story
A traditional rice delicacy found across the Philippines.
Where to try
Public markets, kakanin stalls, bakeries, and local fiestas.

Quezon
05 / A LOCAL EXPERIENCE

Pancit Habhab

Lucban's famous stir-fried noodles combine miki Lucban with pork and vegetables. Served on banana leaves with vinegar, it is traditionally eaten without utensils.

Food story
A well-loved merienda, especially during the Pahiyas Festival.
Where to try
Lucban, Quezon, including local panciterias.

The story behind our identity

Meaning of Our Logo

Our logo represents the flavors, traditions, and cultural identity of CALABARZON. Each element reflects our goal of promoting local delicacies and preserving the region's culinary heritage.

Food and Culinary Heritage

The food-inspired identity represents the diverse delicacies of CALABARZON and our appreciation for traditional Filipino cuisine.

Nature and Local Ingredients

The green theme symbolizes nature, freshness, and the local ingredients that contribute to the region's distinctive flavors.

Warmth and Filipino Hospitality

Warm colors represent the passion, friendliness, and hospitality associated with sharing food and welcoming visitors.

Unity and Regional Identity

The overall identity brings together the five provinces of Cavite, Laguna, Batangas, Rizal, and Quezon through their shared love for food and cultural traditions.

The Name “Lasang Timog”

“Lasang” refers to taste or flavor, while “Timog” means south. Together, the name celebrates the flavors and culinary heritage of Southern Luzon.
